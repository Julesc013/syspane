## Exact prepared state

After ordinary full recovery reconstruction, the detached RecoveryWork adopts its
candidate locally and evaluates preview and commit eligibility using the existing
SettingsDraft implementation. Preserve structural validation outcomes only, with
the exact candidate and existing opaque draft/profile/generation validity proof.
The result has at most two optional booleans; it grants no permission and carries
no new command, authority, request identity, mutable catalog or scheduler.

Only successful changed-state consumption may transfer these structural hints to
the live SettingsDraft, after its ordinary recovery permission, pristine-state,
identity and token checks and existing bounded history adoption. No-op restoration
keeps its original behavior. Capture preparation and synchronous restore retain
their existing semantics. Selection changes remain allowed between preparation
and restore and are recorded by Undo at consumption time.

A valid restored draft may be uncommittable under current policy. Do not refuse
restoration merely because commit eligibility is false. Every eligible live query
still authorizes the current policy/resources. Policy, baseline, authored state,
request state and lifetime changes retain their existing invalidation behavior.
Apply still constructs and fully validates its actual command through begin();
prepared UI hints cannot skip that validation or change tickets, revisions or
durability. Failed or stale consumption must not import hints into another draft.

The existing serialized native worker owns this additional pure preparation.
Cancellation and shutdown keep their original slot/acknowledgment semantics.
There is no new worker, queue, timer or increase to product or workspace limits.
Rendering, native preview validation, geometry and production gates are unchanged.
